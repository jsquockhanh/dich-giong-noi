CAP NHAT DQT DICH TRUC TIEP

Da cap nhat trong goi nay:
1. Them nut "Doi chieu hoi thoai" tren man hinh chinh.
   - Khi nguoi dung chon nguon la Tieng Viet va dich sang ngon ngu doi thoai, co the bam nut khi doi luot noi.
   - Nut hoan doi ngon ngu nguon/dich nhanh; neu nguon dang de tu dong, lan bam dau tien chuyen huong dich ve Tieng Viet.
   - Danh muc ngon ngu co san cua du an bao gom Trung, Nhat, Han, Anh, Phap va nhieu ngon ngu khac.
2. Giam thoi gian cho truoc khi day cau noi dich bang giong tuy chon tu 1200ms xuong 650ms.
3. Giu nguyen cac cau hinh, khoa API, dich vu va cac chuc nang khac.

GIOI HAN CAN BIET:
- Goi nay chua co tinh nang chup man hinh/OCR theo vung chon va vuot chuyen vung. Phan nay can bo sung pipeline MediaProjection + OCR + giao dien chon vung rieng; khong duoc gia lap la da hoat dong.
- Che do hoi thoai hien tai la doi chieu thu cong bang nut, chua tu dong phat hien ai dang noi va tu dong dao chieu.
- Chua xuat APK. Thu build tai moi truong nay khong tai duoc Gradle distribution do moi truong khong co ket noi mang (UnknownHostException: services.gradle.org). Hay dung GitHub Actions de build va gui log neu co loi.
